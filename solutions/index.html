Questions

Common questions

Do you need access to our real data?

No. We design and test with made-up examples that look like yours. When the software runs on your computers, your data stays there.

What does a first project look like?

One process, end to end. We map it with you, then build a working prototype on your own hardware so you can judge it on real work.

Can it work without internet?

Usually, yes. Reading documents, search, masking and most AI tasks can run offline. Work that drives a website, such as a government portal, needs a connection while it runs.

Do you support Bangla?

Yes, from the start: Bangla interfaces, Bangla OCR, search that works across Bangla and English, and conversion of old Bijoy text.
