eGP AutoPilot is a desktop assistant for Bangladesh's e-Government Procurement portal (eprocure.gov.bd). Enter your company's details once; it fills, encrypts, checks and maps documents for every tender in your list, and tells you exactly what's left.

The problem

The same forms, for every tender, every time.

For each e-GP tender a firm must log in, fill the same tenderer forms again, run the portal's encryption, check every row and attach the same documents to the right slots. Firms bidding on dozens of tenders spend whole days on this, and one missed row can mean a missed deadline.

Fills every tender form

Tenderer information, activities, personnel, priced BOQ and quoted forms, from details you enter once.

Runs the portal's encryption

Clicks through the portal's own sign, encrypt and save steps for each form. It never bypasses the portal's checks.

Checks its own work

A verification pass re-scans the form list and catches any Fill or Encrypt button that was missed.

Maps your documents

Matches your files to the portal's categories (trade licence, TIN, VAT, liquid assets) even when file names differ.

Recovers from interruptions

If the internet drops or the app closes, it waits, then resumes at the exact tender and form where it stopped.

Many tenders, many accounts

Works through every tender in your preparation list, and keeps each portal account's settings separate.

Bangla and English

The whole app switches between Bangla and English with one click.

Watch from your phone

Pair a phone with a QR code to see live progress and start or stop a run while you are away from the desk.

How it works

Set it up once, then let it run.

  1. Sign in and choose your portal account. Add as many e-GP accounts as your plan allows.
  2. Enter your details once. Tenderer information, form values and your document files, saved for every future tender.
  3. Start the run. It opens the portal in Chrome or Firefox and works through each tender: fill, encrypt, verify, map.
  4. Read the summary. Each failing row gets several retries; anything still unfinished is listed so you can finish it by hand. Tenders already submitted are skipped.

What it does not do

  • It does not choose which tenders to bid on or write your prices.
  • It does not bypass the portal's encryption or validation; it uses the portal's own screens.
  • It works with eprocure.gov.bd only.

Your data

  • Your e-GP portal password, form values and documents stay in the app on your computer.
  • Your eGP AutoPilot account (email), plan and payment records are kept by us to manage your subscription.
  • Passwords are hidden from the phone dashboard by default.

Plans

Start with a free trial.

After the trial, choose a monthly, 3-month, 6-month or yearly plan, paid by bKash. Plans differ in how many computers and portal accounts you can use and whether phone access is included. Buying a new plan while one is running keeps your remaining days. Larger firms can ask about an enterprise plan.

Requirements

  • A Windows or Mac computer.
  • Google Chrome (used automatically if installed) or Firefox.
  • An eprocure.gov.bd tenderer account.
  • An internet connection while the run is working on the portal.
