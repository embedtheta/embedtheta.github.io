Why it exists

Your answers are already in your files. Finding them is the problem.

Offices keep years of letters, reports, scanned orders and spreadsheets in mixed Bangla and English, some typed in old Bijoy fonts that ordinary search can't read. Cloud tools mean uploading confidential files. Context Extractor reads them all on your own computer.

Reads almost every office file

PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel and CSV, text, web pages and images, across a folder or a whole drive.

Bangla and English OCR

Scanned pages and photos are read with OCR, only where a page has no usable text, so large scans stay fast.

Old Bijoy files, fixed

Text typed in Bijoy (SutonnyMJ) is converted to Unicode, so decades-old documents become searchable.

Search by word or by meaning

Exact keyword search that handles Bangla well, plus meaning search that finds a passage in Bangla when you ask in English, and the reverse.

Ask questions, get sources

Chat with your documents through an AI model on your own computer. Every answer cites the file and page it came from.

Completely offline

Reading, search and chat all work without an internet connection. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Big jobs, done in the background

See progress, pause and resume, and re-read only the files that changed since last time.

Names and places index

People, organisations, places and dates mentioned across all your documents, collected in one list.

How it works

Three steps, no setup.

  1. Choose a folder or drive. It skips system folders, empty files and Office lock files.
  2. Let it read. Every file is read once and indexed on your computer. You can keep working meanwhile.
  3. Search or ask. Smart, exact or meaning search, or ask a question about all files or just the ones you pick.

Status

Reading, OCR, Bijoy conversion, keyword and meaning search, and chat with cited answers are working. We are finishing the import of data from the earlier version and testing the Bijoy conversion on real archives.

Good to know

  • Built for Windows; a fast PC with plenty of disk space helps with very large archives.
  • The chat model is a separate download (about 5 GB for the fast model).
  • Old .doc and .ppt files need LibreOffice installed.
