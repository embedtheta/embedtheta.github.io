Questions

Does my text go to EmbedTheta?

No. ThetaMask runs entirely in your browser. It has no servers, no account and no analytics, and it makes no network requests. We could not see your text if we wanted to.

Won't fake names make the AI's answer worse?

Usually not. ThetaMask swaps real details for realistic ones of the same kind (a name for a name, a UK phone number for a UK phone number), so the AI writes a normal answer. When it comes back, ThetaMask shows you the real details in place of the stand-ins.

Which AI tools does it work with?

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini in Chrome and Edge today. Firefox, Microsoft Copilot and a desktop app for other tools are planned.

What does it miss?

It is very good, not perfect. In our latest tests it caught all 38 sensitive values in 15 new real-world prompts, and 118 of 119 in our main test set. A company named on its own with no clue around it can slip through, so add your clients to your own always-hide list (it takes a minute). Files and images you upload are not checked yet.

Is this enough for GDPR or client confidentiality rules?

It is a strong safeguard, not legal advice. Pair it with a written AI-use policy (our free generator writes a first draft) and your firm's usual review.

What will it cost?

The browser extension is free for individuals. A team plan for firms, with shared word lists and settings an IT person can roll out, is planned at about $4 per person per month. Beta firms use everything free.
