Beta programme

Help shape ThetaMask before it launches.

We're inviting 20 firms of 5 to 200 people to use ThetaMask free from now until launch in February 2027, and to tell us what works and what doesn't.

What you get

  • Free use for everyone at your firm during the beta.
  • A direct line to us: bugs you report get fixed first.
  • Your client names and code words set up with you, in a 15-minute call if you want one.
  • A first-draft AI-use policy for your firm.

What we ask

  • At least two people use it in their normal AI work for two weeks.
  • Tell us when it misses something or gets in the way. A one-line email is enough.
  • A 20-minute call at the end of the two weeks.

We never see your data

ThetaMask runs on your computers and sends nothing to us. When you report a problem, describe it or use made-up details; please never send us real client information.

Applications open shortly. In the meantime, try the live demo or write your firm's AI-use policy.
