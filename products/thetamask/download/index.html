Install in Chrome or Edge

  1. Download the beta .zip file and unzip it (double-click on a Mac; right-click, Extract All on Windows). You get a folder called thetamask-0.7.0-chrome. Keep it somewhere it won't be deleted, such as your Documents folder: the browser loads ThetaMask from there.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge: edge://extensions) in the address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the thetamask-0.7.0-chrome folder.
  5. The ThetaMask welcome page opens. You're protected straight away.
  6. Optional: click the puzzle-piece icon and pin ThetaMask so its shield is always visible.

Chrome may show a reminder about developer-mode extensions when it starts; that's normal for beta builds and goes away once ThetaMask is in the store.

What it needs

  • Chrome or Edge 116 or newer, on a desktop or laptop.
  • About 70 MB of disk space (most of it the on-device AI model).
  • No account, no sign-up inside the extension, no internet connection for it to work.
