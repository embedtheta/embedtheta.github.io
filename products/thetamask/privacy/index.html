Privacy policy

ThetaMask privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. Applies to the ThetaMask browser extension, made by EmbedTheta. For this website, see the website privacy notice.

Summary

ThetaMask does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It works entirely on your computer and never sends us anything.

The extension

What it processes

To protect you, the extension reads the text you paste or type into the message box on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, and the answers shown on those pages. It does this only on those three websites, and only on your computer.

What it stores, on your computer only

  • Settings and word lists you enter (for example client names to always hide), in your browser's local extension storage, until you change or remove them or uninstall the extension.
  • Each chat's table of real details and their stand-ins, in your browser's session storage. It is deleted when you close the browser, or when you choose "Forget all chats now" in Settings.
  • Weekly counts of how many items of each kind were hidden per day (numbers only, never the details themselves), kept for 35 days. You can reset them in Settings.

What it sends

Nothing to us or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests. The only text that leaves your computer is the message you choose to send to the AI service, with the protected details already replaced by stand-ins. That service's own privacy policy applies to what you send it.

Emails you send us

If you email us (for example to join the beta), we use your email and what you tell us only to reply and to run the beta, and we delete it on request. Please never send us real client information.

Children

ThetaMask is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe the change here. A change that would collect any data would require your clear permission first.

Contact

Questions: contact details will appear here when the beta opens.
