Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers; even the font is served from this site. The ThetaMask demo and the AI policy generator run entirely in your browser, and what you type into them is not sent anywhere.

GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, run trials and beta programmes, and support you. We delete them on request. Please never send us real client or tender data.

Our products

Each product handles data differently, so each has its own notice:

  • ThetaMask: collects nothing. Everything happens in your browser.
  • eGP AutoPilot: your portal password, form values and documents stay on your computer. Your app account (email), plan, devices and payment records are kept by us to run your subscription.
  • Context Extractor: reads, indexes and answers questions about your files entirely on your computer, without an internet connection.
