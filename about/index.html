What we believe

Most teams that would gain the most from AI are the ones that can least afford a data leak: law and accounting firms, contractors bidding on government work, offices holding years of confidential files. Cloud AI asks them to choose between speed and safety.

We think they shouldn't have to. Modern AI models are now small enough to run on an ordinary laptop. So we put the intelligence where the data already is, on your computer, and keep what leaves it to a minimum.

Where we're headed

Edge AI means running intelligence where data is created: on a laptop today, and on embedded hardware tomorrow. Our name says it: we embed small, efficient AI into the devices people already use. Our software products are the first step; embedded systems and edge AI devices, alongside custom software solutions, are the next.

How we work

  • Local first. If a job can run on your computer, it does.
  • Bangla is not an afterthought. Interfaces, OCR and search are built for Bangla and English from the start, including old Bijoy text.
  • Honest about limits. Each product page says what the software does not do, and our test results are published.
  • One job, done properly. Every product finishes a whole task, recovers from interruptions and tells you what's left.
