Tender preparation, automated end to end

Built for: Contractors and procurement agents bidding on Bangladesh e-GP tenders · In paid use · version 2.2, 2026

20

paying customers

4

steps automated per tender: fill, encrypt, verify, map

2

platforms: Windows and macOS

The problem
For every tender, staff re-typed the same company forms, ran the portal's encryption and attached the same documents by hand. Firms bidding on dozens of tenders lost whole days, and a single missed row could cost a deadline.
What was built
A Bangla and English desktop assistant that fills, encrypts, verifies and maps documents for every tender in the list, retries failed rows, resumes after a dropped connection and reports exactly what is left.
