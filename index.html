How a project works

From a conversation to software on your desk.

  1. Free consultation

    A call about one process that takes your team too long. Made-up examples only.

  2. Written proposal

    Scope, timeline and estimate in writing, before any work starts.

  3. Working prototype

    On your own machines, early in the project, tested on real tasks.

  4. Deploy

    Installed on your computers, offline where possible, with training for your team.

  5. Support

    Updates when portals, formats or rules change.

Questions

Questions clients ask us

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the process. Every project starts with a free consultation, then a written proposal with a fixed scope and estimate before any work begins.

How long does it take?

It depends on the process; your written proposal gives the timeline. You see a working prototype early, because we automate one process end to end before expanding.

Do you need access to our real data?

No. We build and test with made-up examples that look like yours. When the software runs on your computers, your data stays there.

Where are you based? Do you work with clients abroad?

We are based in Dhaka, Bangladesh and work remotely with clients in Bangladesh and abroad. Our founder has industry experience in Japan.

What if a portal or file format changes later?

We support what we build. When a website or format changes, we update the software so your team can keep working.
