How we build

Software you can trust with the files that matter.

Runs on your computer

Our AI runs on the machine in front of you. Client files, tender documents and prompts stay there instead of travelling to someone else's servers.

Bangla and English, properly

Bilingual interfaces, Bangla OCR, and even old Bijoy (SutonnyMJ) text converted to Unicode, because that is what real office files contain.

Built around real work

Each product starts from one slow, error-prone job a team does every week, and does it end to end: filling, checking, recovering when the internet drops.

Works when the internet doesn't

Search, masking and document reading keep working offline. Long jobs save their progress and pick up exactly where they stopped.

Where we're going

From the desktop to the device.

Our products already run compact AI models locally: a 46 MB model that spots names in your browser, multilingual search and chat that work without internet. The same approach carries over to embedded hardware, where AI has to be small, fast and private by design.
