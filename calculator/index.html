About the estimate

How accurate is this estimate?

It uses your own figures and a conservative share of the work being automated. Real savings depend on the process; after a free consultation, your written proposal gives an estimate for your actual task.

Why not 100% automated?

Most business processes still need a person to check results, handle exceptions and make decisions. We design for that, so the estimate starts low.

Is the value cash I will save?

Not necessarily. It is the value of staff time freed for other work. Whether that becomes cash savings depends on how you use the time.
